Vendor Statement Reconciliation – Decision Map for the Analyst

Use this interactive guide to identify differences between the vendor statement and the ERP, select the appropriate action, document the outcome, and follow up until the item is resolved.
Initial question

Decision path

    Update the reconciliation tracker

    Cleared
    No differences remain.
    Difference explained / action taken
    The item is understood and the required action has been completed or documented.
    Pending
    Waiting for vendor response, requester action, approval, payment action, or follow-up.
    If the type of difference is not clear, consult your Coordinator or OpCo and retain the supporting evidence used during the analysis.

    Roles involved

    AnalystPerforms the reconciliation, investigates differences, takes the required processing actions, and documents the outcome.
    CoordinatorSupports the analyst, provides guidance, and helps escalate or resolve unclear cases.
    OpCoReviews, comments, approves where required, and supports resolution of operational issues.

    Possible questions to consider

    Invoice differences

    • Is the invoice recorded in the ERP?
    • Was the invoice received but not posted?
    • Is it linked to the correct PO / receipt?
    • Is there a related credit note?

    Payment differences

    • Is the invoice due?
    • Is it awaiting the next payment run?
    • Is there a payment block?
    • Was the payment reversed or applied incorrectly?

    Credit differences

    • Was the credit recorded internally?
    • Is the related invoice known?
    • Was the credit applied to the correct invoice?
    • Can the remaining balance be applied or refunded?

    Other differences

    • Is there an exchange-rate difference?
    • Are there duplicate invoice lines or records?
    • Are there unreported charges or adjustments?
    • Is supporting documentation missing?
    This decision map is a quick-reference aid. Each case may require supporting documentation, stakeholder confirmation, and follow-up according to the applicable process.
